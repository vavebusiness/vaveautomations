OUR APPROACH

Your workflow determines the solution.

We work with business tools such as Make, n8n, APIs, Google Workspace and other appropriate systems. Technology comes after the problem, not before it.

STEP 01

Tell us what's difficult

Describe one workflow you repeat, where it gets stuck, and who it affects. You don't need to know how to automate it.

STEP 02

We map the process

We ask about your existing software, inputs, exceptions, data access and what success would mean for your team.

STEP 03

Then we scope a build

If automation makes sense, we outline what would be built, what must be tested, the limitations and the proposed cost. No work begins without agreement.
