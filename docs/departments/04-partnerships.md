# 04 — Partnerships

> **Vave Automations operational brief** · Reviewed 2 November 2026 · Owner: Partner development
>
> Public-safe process documentation. The private master CRM and authenticated connected services remain authoritative.

## Mission and outcome

**Mission:** Build credible relationships with teams that actually need technical capacity or referral help.

**Definition of success:** Specific commercial collaboration with clear scope and responsibilities.

## Current mandate

- Potential future integration partner: waiting
- ERP agency: verified no current external need
- White-label posting: portfolio mismatch, hold
- No signed paid partner engagements

## Standard operating procedure

1. **Identify** — Verify agencies offering adjacent services or publishing concrete overflow requests.
2. **Assess** — Check whether demand is current, technical fit is credible, and an allowed contact route exists.
3. **Introduce** — Describe actual skills and independently tested work without invented customer references.
4. **Define** — Clarify client ownership, confidentiality, commercial handoffs, expectations and payment.
5. **Pilot** — Accept only bounded tasks with access, test cases and clear approval.
6. **Review** — Track real referrals and outcomes rather than generic networking metrics.

## Decision and safety rules

- No fake case studies or testimonials
- Respect confirmed internal delivery teams and declines
- Do not bypass forum posting restrictions
