> Public-safe process documentation. The private master CRM and authenticated connected services remain authoritative.

## Mission and outcome

**Mission:** Make the right decisions using reliable status across every specialist team.

**Definition of success:** Accurate, current executive operating picture.

## Current mandate

- First verified paying client, not gross outreach volume
- Real opportunities and next actions
- Inter-department handoffs
- Truthful reporting and evidence

## Standard operating procedure

1. **Collect** — Review CRM Dashboard, Sent Outreach, Opportunities, Gmail, relevant Make executions and automation reports.
2. **Reconcile** — Separate accepted sends from distinct recipients, and replies from buyer-qualified conversations.
3. **Decide** — Rank opportunities based on verified pain, contact permission, urgency, feasibility and potential commercial impact.
4. **Assign** — Give each action a responsible department, evidence link and appropriate review date.
5. **Close the loop** — Record only verified updates; note exceptions, unavailable data and commitments that remain unresolved.

## Decision and safety rules

- Keep direct buyer requests higher priority than generic research
- Hold buyer follow-up where last useful check-in has already been sent
- Keep Make production scenarios inactive until approval and testing

- Qualified buyer conversations
