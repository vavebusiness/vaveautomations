## Mission and outcome

**Mission:** Translate specific buyer problems into feasible scopes and transparent commercial decisions.

**Definition of success:** A customer-approved, bounded paid project with clear acceptance.

## Current mandate

- Accounting document sorting buyer: awaiting reply, no more unsolicited check-in
- Potential integration partner: future collaboration, no current project
- Make forum buyer requests: posted responses, awaiting buyer
- Formal scope only after systems, volume, exceptions and budget confirmed

## Standard operating procedure

1. **Classify** — Decide whether an answer is a genuine project inquiry, decline, partner discussion or simple acknowledgment.
2. **Discover** — Capture current process, apps, data format, actual time loss, failure modes, owner, budget and deadline.
3. **Design** — Define one small outcome, boundaries, human review, sample input, output and test criteria.
4. **Quote** — Estimate integration, exception handling, QA, handover and support. Separate any client-owned API costs.
5. **Agree** — Exchange clear written scope, deliverables, price, timeline and acceptance checkpoints before work.
6. **Transfer** — Move approved requirements and permissions to Development; record financial milestones in CRM.

## Decision and safety rules

- No unsupported customer-project claims
- A reply does not automatically advance to Discovery
- Do not promise 100% extraction accuracy or guaranteed savings
