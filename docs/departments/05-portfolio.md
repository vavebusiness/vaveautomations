> Public-safe process documentation. The private master CRM and authenticated connected services remain authoritative.

## Mission and outcome

**Mission:** Help buyers understand capabilities through truthful technical examples and clear documentation.

**Definition of success:** Consistent, evidence-backed materials that shorten discovery and delivery.

## Current mandate

- Rent roll/document-processing prototype evidence
- Discovery and paid pilot template
- Delivery QA and handover runbook
- No unrelated product-store projects

## Standard operating procedure

1. **Inventory** — Separate private prototypes, finished internal documents and actual customer projects.
2. **Validate** — Read the real scenario, execution history and QA notes before describing capabilities.
3. **Document** — Create input/output diagram, scope boundaries, setup requirements and known limitations.
4. **Sanitize** — Use synthetic examples; remove secrets, customer documents, personal identifiers and contact data.
5. **Approve** — Ensure sales claims exactly match evidence and don't imply a customer deployment.
6. **Maintain** — Version each public or buyer-facing asset and note review dates.

## Decision and safety rules

- Never treat unrelated product workflows as Vave Automations projects
- No published success claim without acceptance evidence
- No credentials, client data or private screenshots in public assets
