# 01 — Client Acquisition

> **Vave Automations operational brief** · Reviewed 2 November 2026 · Owner: Prospecting
>
> Public-safe process documentation. The private master CRM and authenticated connected services remain authoritative.

## Mission and outcome

**Mission:** Identify businesses with actual, evidence-backed automation demand and a legitimate contact route.

**Definition of success:** Buyer-quality leads and booked discovery, not arbitrary send counts.

## Current mandate

- Direct Make/n8n project requests with explicit scope
- Accounting document intake where friction is actually evidenced
- Integration agency overflow and subcontract demand
- Verified contact route and safe suppression handling

## Standard operating procedure

1. **Source** — Find a recent, explicit work request or documented manual process in a reachable business.
2. **Verify** — Check source date, organization, tools, gap, buyer role and whether demand remains open.
3. **Deduplicate** — Compare organization, domain, recipient and prior thread against Opportunities, Sent Outreach and No Contact.
4. **Score** — Consider pain evidence, scope fit, urgency, accessible contact and risk before allocating outreach.
5. **Contact responsibly** — Ask one grounded question through an allowed route; track accepted delivery versus rejected attempt.
6. **Handoff** — Log the exact source, conversation evidence, current status and next date for Sales.

## Decision and safety rules

- Do not assume online document exchange is inefficient
- Do not write prohibited AI-authored forum posts
- Never retry suppressed or bounced addresses

## Metrics and evidence

- Qualified-to-contacted ratio
- Positive reply rate by channel
- Accepted and bounced sends separately
- Recent buyer requests versus stale listings

Metrics are defined here; report them as verified only after checking source timestamps and records.

## Department handoffs

1. Research → Qualification → Contacted → Reply verified
2. Qualified buyer reply → Sales discovery
3. Negative or bounce → Operations suppression
